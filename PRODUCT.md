# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

android

## Users
Warkari devotees who read or sing abhangs, aartis and Haripath at home, in bhajan gatherings or on a wari, often one-handed or in dim light. Users read Marathi (Devanagari); many type in Latin letters because Devanagari input is awkward.

## Product Purpose
A fully offline Android app for browsing and reading Marathi abhangs (Tukaram, Namdev, Dnyaneshwar's Haripath, aartis, Pasaydan and others) organised into 9 categories. Success: a devotee finds the verse they want quickly, reads it clearly, and can trust the text.

## Positioning
Works entirely offline with a large bundled corpus (about 4,500 Tukaram gatha plus Haripath, aartis, Namdev and sant verses), and phonetic search lets users type Marathi in English letters ("vitthal" finds "विठ्ठल").

## Operating Context
Content is generated by a Node pipeline from Marathi Wikisource and a few other public sources, with a human review loop for auto-repaired text. Some abhangs are marked as auto-corrected and need verification; the text of Haripath, Pasaydan and aartis varies between traditions.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Native Kotlin + Jetpack Compose + Material 3, single module, minSdk 24, no network access.
- Screens: home (categories), category list with title/number filter, detail, search, about/sources.
- All UI strings and content are Marathi. Bundled font: Google Sans (OFL).
- Content licensing: public-domain / CC BY-SA sources, credited in the About screen.

## Brand Commitments
Name: Abhang. Marathi-first voice. No other identity assets exist yet.

## Evidence on Hand
Real corpus in `app/src/main/assets/abhangs.json`; source texts in `content/raw/`. No testimonials, user data or store metrics exist; do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles
- Reading and singing come first: legible Devanagari, fast access, no friction.
- Fully offline, always: never depend on a connection.
- Honest about text quality: surface provenance and unreviewed status plainly.
- Search should forgive spelling and script.
- Respect the devotional context; avoid gimmicks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Devanagari must render reliably at large text sizes and in low light. No further product-specific standard established.
