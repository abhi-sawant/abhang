# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

android

## Users
Warkari devotees who read or sing abhangs, aartis and Haripath at home, in bhajan gatherings or on a wari, often one-handed or in dim light. Users read Marathi (Devanagari); many type in Latin letters because Devanagari input is awkward.

## Product Purpose
A fully offline Android app for browsing and reading Marathi abhangs (Tukaram, Namdev, Dnyaneshwar's Haripath, aartis, Pasaydan and others) organised into 9 categories. Success: a devotee finds the verse they want quickly, reads it clearly, and can trust the text.

## Positioning
Works entirely offline with a large bundled corpus: 4,476 texts, mostly the Tukaram gatha (about 4,340), plus about 100 Namdev abhangs, Dnyaneshwar's Haripath and Pasaydan, two aartis and short gajar chants. Phonetic search lets users type Marathi in English letters ("vitthal" finds "विठ्ठल").

## Operating Context
Content is generated by a Node pipeline from Marathi Wikisource and a few other public sources, with a human review loop for auto-repaired text. Most Tukaram abhangs (about 3,600) are still marked as auto-corrected and need verification; reviewed ones are marked "तपासले". The text of Haripath, Pasaydan and aartis varies between traditions and is marked to be checked against the reader's own tradition.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Native Kotlin + Jetpack Compose + Material 3, single module, minSdk 24. No INTERNET permission; voice search hands off to the system speech recognizer, which may need a connection if no offline Marathi pack is installed.
- Screens: home (categories, continue reading, favourites), category list with title/number filter, detail (adjustable font size, favourite, share, screen kept on, provenance badge), favourites, search (typed or voice), about/sources with app version.
- Light and dark themes follow the system setting.
- All UI strings and content are Marathi. Bundled font: Google Sans (OFL).
- Content licensing: traditional public-domain texts; Wikisource material is CC BY-SA. All sources are credited in the About screen.

## Brand Commitments
Name: Abhang; the launcher label is "अभंग संग्रह". Marathi-first voice. The only identity asset is the adaptive launcher icon.

## Evidence on Hand
Real corpus in `app/src/main/assets/abhangs.json`; source texts in `content/raw/`. No testimonials, user data or store metrics exist; do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles
- Reading and singing come first: legible Devanagari, fast access, no friction.
- Fully offline, always: never depend on a connection.
- Honest about text quality: surface provenance and unreviewed status plainly.
- Search should forgive spelling and script.
- Respect the devotional context; avoid gimmicks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Devanagari must render reliably at large text sizes and in low light. The reading font size can be changed in the app and also respects the system font scale, up to a cap. No further product-specific standard established.
