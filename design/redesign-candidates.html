Abhang: three redesign candidates

Static comps of the four core screens: home, a category list, the reading view and search. All copy and verses are real, taken from the bundled corpus. Each candidate replaces the current saffron-and-cream look with its own world. Pick one (or mix a named piece from another) and I'll implement it in Compose.

A · पोथी (the manuscript) B · वारी (the road) C · गाभारा (the sanctum)

A · पोथी Pothi, the rubricated manuscript

The app becomes a hand-bound pothi. Turmeric-dyed paper (the real traditional pest-proofing), black ink, and kumkum-red for everything a scribe would rubricate: verse numbers, rules, and the poet's own signature line. The contents page is a true table of contents with dotted leaders.

Scene
Daylight and lamp-lit rooms, reading from a printed-feeling page. Highest comfort for long sung reading.
Palette
#F3DB82 paper#F8E8A9 leaf#21160A ink#9E1B13 kumkum
Type
Tiro Devanagari Marathi for titles and verses (a Marathi-form serif), Mukta for numerals and controls.
Signature
The तुका म्हणे / ज्ञानदेव म्हणे line is detected and set in red; couplets break at the । caesura; double-rule frames edge every bar.
Honest risk
Most ornamental. Heavy yellow can tire eyes at night, so it needs a dark twin (ink-brown paper, parchment text). Rubricating the signature relies on a "म्हणे" heuristic.

B · वारी Wari, the road to Pandharpur

Browsing is a journey. The nine categories are stops on a saffron route in the order a bhajan actually travels, from invocation to Pasaydan. Every abhang number is a milestone stone. Built for glare: white cotton ground, indigo ink, a pataka-saffron line, tulsi-green counts.

Scene
Outdoors on the wari or in a bright hall, one-handed, sun on the screen. Highest contrast of the three.
Palette
#F7F8F4 cotton#111A36 indigo#EE6F12 pataka#1B6B43 tulsi
Type
Yatra One for titles (sign-painter weight), Mukta for verses and UI. One sans family reads fastest at 24sp+.
Signature
The route on Home; milestone-stone numbers on lists; a standard 3-tab Material navigation bar (अभंग · शोधा · माहिती) replaces the about strip; sticky reading controls at the thumb.
Honest risk
Boldest and least "devotional-quiet". Yatra One is loud at small sizes, so it is limited to titles. Saffron cannot carry text on white (contrast), only lines and fills.

C · गाभारा Gabhara, the sanctum at night

Vithoba is सावळा, the dark one, and bhajan happens after dusk. A Chandrabhaga-indigo ground with one marigold lamp-colour and warm cotton-white text. Search and filter sit in the thumb zone at the bottom; while reading, the couplet you are singing is lit and the rest recede.

Scene
Night bhajan, dim rooms, phone held in one hand. Low glare, warm (not blue) whites.
Palette
#0B111F night#1C2742 stone#EFE9DA cotton#FFB22E lamp
Type
Laila (calligraphic Devanagari) for titles and verses at weight 500+, Hind for UI.
Signature
Bottom search bar with the "vitthal → विठ्ठल" echo above it, and line-by-line reading focus: tap advances the lit couplet, the others dim.
Honest risk
Needs a light twin for daylight. The focus-dimming mode is new behaviour (a state per screen), and Laila's thin strokes need the 500 weight floor for legibility.
